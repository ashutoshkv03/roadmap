Deliberately left off

These show up on the full roadmap.sh backend map, but you don't need them to land an entry-level backend job. Learn them on the job if your team uses them.

  • A second backend language, until you've gone deep in one
  • Deep Kubernetes (Helm, operators, service meshes like Istio)
  • Terraform and infrastructure-as-code beyond reading a simple file
  • CQRS, event sourcing, and domain-driven design in depth
  • Every NoSQL family (graph, wide-column, time-series) beyond knowing they exist
  • Serverless architecture as its own topic, SOAP, and XML-based APIs
  • Search engines like Elasticsearch beyond a one-line "what it's for"
  • Frontend frameworks; basic HTML/JSON familiarity is enough
